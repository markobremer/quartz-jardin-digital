> Today <a href="https://twitter.com/molpsychiatry">@molpsychiatry</a> released an interesting series of critiques of the controversial umbrella review which found no consistent evidence of there being an association between serotonin and depression by <a href="https://twitter.com/joannamoncrieff">@joannamoncrieff</a> <a href="https://twitter.com/HengartnerMP">@hengartnermp</a> <a href="https://twitter.com/markhoro">@markhoro</a> 🧵1/7

#psiquiatría #depresión #serotonina #investigación 

> First, <a href="https://twitter.com/darnone9">@darnone9</a> <a href="https://twitter.com/toby_wise">@toby_wise</a> <a href="https://twitter.com/PBFitzgerald">@PBFitzgerald</a> argue that the involvement of serotonin in the pathophysiology of depression in a more complex manner is very likely, rather than the simplistic serotonin model of depression. https://t.co/9OIblnWNKw 2/7 
> ![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/GGtso9mWMAQ2WcL.png)
 ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/NTFabiano/status/1759633476859347390))

> Second, <a href="https://twitter.com/smittytoo">@smittytoo</a> <a href="https://twitter.com/solmi_marco">@solmi_marco</a> point out methodological shortcomings which they posit compromise the transparency and reproducibility of findings presented. https://t.co/bxiPQOKdXX 3/7 
> ![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/GGtqgaTWMAMo1WC.png)
 ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/NTFabiano/status/1759633479283663213))

> Third, Darya Ahmed references the importance of the biopsychosocial model to explain mental illnesses, stating that the current umbrella review did not address the multifactorial etiology of depression. https://t.co/EDQCAiOcdo 4/7 
> ![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/GGtsDpTWMAU-SfM.png)
 ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/NTFabiano/status/1759633481338933472))

> Fourth, Konstantinos Fountoulakis & Eva Tsapakis agree that the serotonin theory for depression is likely incorrect as the core biological dysfunction in depression, however state that findings do not support a non-biological causation. https://t.co/F9XBsfVB1Q 5/7 
> ![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/GGtsNb5WAAAmVpf.png)
 ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/NTFabiano/status/1759633483377287664))

> Lastly, in response, <a href="https://twitter.com/joannamoncrieff">@joannamoncrieff</a> <a href="https://twitter.com/HengartnerMP">@hengartnermp</a> <a href="https://twitter.com/markhoro">@markhoro</a> highlight that none of the critiques challenge the conclusion of their umbrella review and address each concern raised. https://t.co/MzgzdX259W 6/7
