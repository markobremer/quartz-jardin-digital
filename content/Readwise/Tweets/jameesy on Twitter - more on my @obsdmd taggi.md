#

> More on my <a href="https://twitter.com/obsdmd">@obsdmd</a> tagging process.
> I will break it down and explain why I think this is the most effective navigation method in Obsidian... 
> Firstly, at a high level, I navigate via 'parent' tags. 
> I have: keyword, type, area, status & brag. 
> ![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/F_hczfHWsAAovKp.jpg)
 ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/jameesy/status/1727235127963697635))



#obsidian 

> These work well for me, but you could have anything here. 
> These parent tags then open up to reveal 'child' tags. 
> (Keyword and area have sensitive things in there, so I won't show those. Primarily peoples names.) 
> ![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/F_hdkTPXoAAq9_M.jpg)
 ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/jameesy/status/1727235131155661166))

> I click on '# keyword/metrics' & it shows all the results that have that tag. 
> I can open/collapse context, filter deeper with extra search terms as well as organise & sort the results. 
> You don't get this level of searchability or manipulation with backlinks or folders.
