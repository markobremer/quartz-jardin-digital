#

> 1/9 Nested tags in #Obsidian allow you to organize your notes with more granularity. Use a format like `#project/subproject` to categorize notes within broader topics. This structure makes it easier to manage and navigate through related notes. #TfT 
> ![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/GHFeGC6aMAAbxW1.jpg)
 ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/TfTHacker/status/1761292394891554979))



#obsidian 

> 2/9 To create a nested tag, add a forward slash between levels (e.g., `#book/chapter1`). This method helps you keep topics neatly organized and logically grouped within your personal knowledge base.
 ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/TfTHacker/status/1761292399253545005))

> 3/9 Benefit of using nested tags? It streamlines finding related notes. Whether working on different project aspects or categorizing research, nested tags help you filter and access your notes quickly.
 ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/TfTHacker/status/1761292402579636717))

> 4/9 The Tags view core plugin in #Obsidian enhances the utility of nested tags. It displays all your tags in a hierarchical view, making it easy to see the structure at a glance and navigate through your tags efficiently. 
> ![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/GHFeHMQbAAAHiOE.jpg)
 ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/TfTHacker/status/1761292411240874380))

> 5/9 To use the Tags view, enable the 'Tags' plugin from the settings. Once activated, you can click on any tag in the sidebar to filter your notes by that tag, including its nested tags, offering a quick way to access all related content. 
> ![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/GHFeHv3bAAAG3Le.jpg)
 ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/TfTHacker/status/1761292420854284622))

> 6/9 Combining nested tags with the Tag view is a powerful way to organize and access your notes. It's like having a dynamic table of contents that adapts to your note-taking needs and grows with your knowledge base.
 ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/TfTHacker/status/1761292425040204116))

> 7/9 Also, if you click on a tag, either in a document or in the Tags view sidepane, Obsidian will do a search on that tag across your vault. 
> ![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/GHFeIfiakAAiURj.jpg)
 ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/TfTHacker/status/1761292434141852097))

> 8/9 Pro tip: Regularly review and adjust your tagging strategy. As your note collection expands, you may find new ways to categorize information, making retrieving and connecting your ideas easier.
 ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/TfTHacker/status/1761292438579315089))
