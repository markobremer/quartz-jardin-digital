> Some people cannot help but see psychotherapy as either a paid reassuring friend OR a teacher who teaches you self-help strategies and advises you OR a technician who performs a technique on you, gives you ketamine and asks questions. Real psychotherapy is none of these things.
 ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/KemtrupTweets/status/1762502629568164030))

> A thing to remember when reading psychotherapy research that is (heavily or entirely) based on self-report and surveys.
> Reducing complaints and reducing suffering are NOT THE SAME thing! You can reduce (or increase) complaints without changing suffering.
 ([View Tweet](https://twitter.com/KemtrupTweets/status/1837892214007255424))

Importante diferenciar los tipos lógicos entre estos resultados.
