> Challenges with misusing strategy tools and templates
> Using strategy tools and templates poorly, by crowd sourcing data and using them haphazardly, can lead to ineffective strategies. For example, using a SWOT analysis template without validating the input can lead to a mix of operational and market issues, where operational issues overshadow market issues. This results in an unclear understanding of strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats in the market due to the lack of validation and customer research.

> Challenges in integrating operational and market issues in strategy development
> Operational issues often take precedence over market issues, hindering the understanding of strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats in the market. The lack of validation in strategic planning leads to uncertainty in identifying strengths and weaknesses. Executives, even in startups, may lack insight into customer needs and behaviors, relying only on sales reports, market research, and quantitative data. This absence of customer insight hinders the formulation of effective strategies.
