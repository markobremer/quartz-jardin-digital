> Balancing intentional focus and open awareness
> The concept of attention involves a balance between intentional focus and open awareness. While focusing on perfecting one's attention as a worker is valuable, a more open form of awareness, such as deepening connections with others, is also important. Trying to control attention too tightly can limit one's ability to notice valuable insights that may arise unexpectedly. The term 'attention' encompasses two distinct projects - intentional focus and open awareness - which can sometimes conflict with each other. By overly instrumentalizing attention, individuals may miss out on the true depth and richness of the world around them.
