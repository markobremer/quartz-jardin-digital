> Epigenetics Unveiled: Understanding Gene Expression
> Epigenetics refers to the mechanisms that influence gene expression, explaining why cells with the same genetic material can differentiate into various cell types, such as liver, kidney, or muscle cells. Each cell type activates specific genes, leading to their unique functions and characteristics. Recently, it has been suggested that these gene activation patterns might be heritable, passing down to subsequent generations. However, the term epigenetics is often misapplied, creating confusion by associating it too broadly with inherited traits rather than accurately reflecting its relation to embryological development.
