#

> Most of us never knew falling in love without swiping and [subscription models](https://substack.com/redirect/8df83ff5-d4ed-4117-9e32-a48b5851a730?j=eyJ1IjoiM2c2ZWE0In0.UBnO9epFHJI2gArKgnhPNpI_1rjXwO8S1S6smvAQsPI). We never knew having a first kiss without having watched PornHub first. We never knew flirting and romance before it became sending DMs or [reacting to Snapchat stories](https://substack.com/redirect/9fa83b76-76f1-4ff5-8529-d2258e41e3ac?j=eyJ1IjoiM2c2ZWE0In0.UBnO9epFHJI2gArKgnhPNpI_1rjXwO8S1S6smvAQsPI) with flame emojis.We never knew friendship before it became [keeping up a Snapstreak](https://substack.com/redirect/9a4d373d-defd-4e64-b4e7-307dd78f557b?j=eyJ1IjoiM2c2ZWE0In0.UBnO9epFHJI2gArKgnhPNpI_1rjXwO8S1S6smvAQsPI) or using each other like props to look popular on Instagram. And the *freedom—*we never felt the freedom to grow up clumsily; to be young and dumb and make stupid mistakes without fear of it being posted online. Or the freedom to be *unavailable,* to disconnect for a while without the pressure of [Read Receipts](https://substack.com/redirect/309fe557-735f-4b81-8df0-121a6a0c4a69?j=eyJ1IjoiM2c2ZWE0In0.UBnO9epFHJI2gArKgnhPNpI_1rjXwO8S1S6smvAQsPI) and *[Last Active](https://substack.com/redirect/5cca6c16-4fb7-4928-976a-d923d8188425?j=eyJ1IjoiM2c2ZWE0In0.UBnO9epFHJI2gArKgnhPNpI_1rjXwO8S1S6smvAQsPI)* statuses. We never knew a childhood spent chasing experiences and risks and independence instead of chasing stupid *likes* on a screen. Never knew life without documenting and marketing and obsessively analyzing it as we went.
