> the success of these publications does not begin to make up for the loss of tens of thousands of journalism jobs over the past two decades. But the newsletter economy *has* managed to do what nothing else has over the same time period — create a popular, lucrative, replicable, stable format for journalism. And in a moment where the industry faces so many threats, from declining traffic and revenue to the assault on free speech from the Trump administration, that’s worth celebrating.
