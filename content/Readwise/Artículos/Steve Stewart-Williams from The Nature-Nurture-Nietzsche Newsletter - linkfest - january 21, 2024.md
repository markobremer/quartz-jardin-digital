> According to a recent study, physical attractiveness is associated with higher educational attainment, greater occupational success, and a higher income, even after controlling for potential confounds such as health, IQ, parental SES, and neighborhood conditions. In other words, it pays to be good looking - literally. Curiously, the benefits of good looks are larger for men than for women, which is the opposite of what I would have predicted
