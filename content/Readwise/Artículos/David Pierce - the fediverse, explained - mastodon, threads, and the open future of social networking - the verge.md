> So what is the fediverse?
> It’s an interconnected social platform ecosystem based on [an open protocol called ActivityPub](https://www.theverge.com/2023/4/20/23689570/activitypub-protocol-standard-social-network), which allows you to port your content, data, and follower graph between networks.
