#

> After rehearsals, some of the singers provided a saliva sample to researchers examining whether singing affected their health and mood — and it did, positively. [The “Sing With Us” study](http://performancescience.ac.uk/singwithus/), which enrolled Hardy and 192 others, is part of a growing body of research that points to the physical and mental health benefits of singing with others. Sing With Us linked singing in the choir to [reduced stress hormones and increased cytokines](https://ecancer.org/en/journal/article/631-singing-modulates-mood-stress-cortisol-cytokine-and-neuropeptide-activity-in-cancer-patients-and-carers/abstract), proteins that can boost the body’s ability to fight serious illness.
