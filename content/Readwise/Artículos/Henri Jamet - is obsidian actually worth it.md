#

> I don’t know if my experience can be generalized to everyone, I’m not here to impose my opinion, or to tell you to rush into it because you won’t regret it. I’m simply here to tell you that, quite frankly, in my particular case, Obsidian has changed my life. My productivity, my desire to write, even my way of seeing the world. 🦥
