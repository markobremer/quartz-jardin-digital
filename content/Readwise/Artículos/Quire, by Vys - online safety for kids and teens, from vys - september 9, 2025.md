#

> On August 31, **the Australian government finally [released](https://substack.com/redirect/59e21691-4c76-4a27-b64a-9958d611fc34?j=eyJ1IjoiM2c2ZWE0In0.UBnO9epFHJI2gArKgnhPNpI_1rjXwO8S1S6smvAQsPI) its findings from its Age Assurance Technology Trial**, run by the Age Check Certification Scheme (ACCS) in advance of the country’s impending under-16 social media ban. To assess the feasibility of age assurance in a privacy and security-preserving manner, the $6.5 million trial tested 60 technologies from 48 different vendors. The report essentially finds that **age assurance “can be done” in Australia without “substantial technological limitations,”** echoing the common understanding of no “one-size-fits-all” solution.
