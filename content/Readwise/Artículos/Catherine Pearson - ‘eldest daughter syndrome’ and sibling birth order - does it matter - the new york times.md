> In larger analyses, the link between birth order and personality traits appears much weaker. [A 2015](https://www.pnas.org/doi/abs/10.1073/pnas.1506451112) study looking at more than 20,000 people in Germany, the United Kingdom and the United States found no link between birth order and personality characteristics — though the researchers did find evidence that older children have a slight advantage in I.Q.
