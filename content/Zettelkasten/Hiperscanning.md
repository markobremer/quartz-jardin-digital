
Práctica de registrar en tiempo real variables neurofisiológicas de agentes interactuando, para analizar los correlatos o efectos de esta interacción.

> *in developmental research, hyperscanning comprises the simultaneous measurement of brain activity in parent/adult and child using different neuroimaging methods, including dual-EEG, dualfNIRS, and dual-MEG or sequential-MEG.* [[@Turk2022-js]]
