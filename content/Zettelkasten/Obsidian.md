Obsidian es un programa específicamente desarrollado para desarrollar sistemas de "Personal Knowledge Management" o [[PKM]].


### Bóvedas abiertas para referencia
- The Sweep by Justin DiRose](https://publish.obsidian.md/justin/_Dashboard). Thoughts on a variety of topics, primarily around productivity, remote work, note-taking, and leisure.
- [Petrichor by Bryan Jenks](https://publish.obsidian.md/bryan-jenks/INDEX). It includes programming and statistic notes organized in a Zettelkasten structure.
- [Moby Diction by David Drysdale](https://publish.obsidian.md/mobydiction/notes/_About). A collection of interlinked notes about strategy, research, UX, product development, learning, and knowledge management.
- [Joschua's Garden](https://joschuasgarden.com/). This is a [digital garden](https://youtu.be/TDqsr3MNTTc) about religion, faith, and book notes. It features a Bible Study Kit with linked notes about the Bible.
- [louis030195 by Louis Beaumont](https://publish.obsidian.md/louis030195/README). A collection of thoughts about computing, philosophy, biology, business, and more. This vault has lots of book notes.
- [Walk in the Forest by Anthony Agbay](https://walkintheforest.com/). This vault is a collection of current in-process notes and completed posts dedicated to exploring biology, chemistry, and medicine with a focus on an interactive exploration through programming environments. It includes some cool ideas for organizing personal knowledge.
- [LYT Kit by Nick Milo](https://publish.obsidian.md/lyt-kit/Umami/The+forest+entrance). This is a starter kit designed to get you immediately hands-on with ways to manage your digital notes. It was quite useful back when I started using Obsidian.
- [Teresa Watts](https://teresawatts.com): Diseñadora UX. Bonita bóveda selfhosted.
